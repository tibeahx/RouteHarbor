      Manual exceptions

      Local destinations remain local. Manual direct rules precede manual bypass rules, lists and detected restrictions. Domain rules are exact unless subdomains are explicitly included.

      Keep “Block protected traffic” selected under path fallback before migrating. Classifier or managed DNS failure enables emergency direct for all destinations. A bypass method failing only affects blocked destinations. Saving does not apply routing.

      Apps using their own encrypted DNS cannot receive domain-specific routing unless their address matches an explicit IP rule. Synthetic managed DNS replies do not support strict DNSSEC validation; URL-only blocks, slowdowns and QUIC-only restrictions are outside detection.

      Session continuity through your relay

      Keep the same relay connection to the destination when an access method changes. Pair your own VPS, keep a ready standby path, and prepare routing after saving. Buffers cover short interruptions within their limits. This does not guarantee uninterrupted calls or a particular switch time.

      Before changing an active relay, its buffers, or prepared access methods, disable continuity and confirm that routing change. Then edit and prepare the new profile. The acknowledgement interval below excludes failure detection and application delay.
